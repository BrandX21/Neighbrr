How It Works

Simple as dropping
a pin.

A completed job becomes a trusted recommendation for the neighborhood.

1
🏠
Homeowner Drops a Pin
After a job, tap your address on the map, tag your contractor, and write a quick review. Takes 2 minutes.
2
📨
Homeowner Shares the Pin
The homeowner shares the pin with the contractor who did the job.
3
✅
Contractor Reviews
The contractor opens the link, reviews the pin, and confirms the job. No app download required.
4
📍
Neighbors Discover
Once confirmed, the pin gets a verified badge so neighbors can discover a contractor someone nearby hired.
